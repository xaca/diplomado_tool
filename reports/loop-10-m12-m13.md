# Loop 10 — M12 Board customization + M13 Notes while presenting

2026-10-31 · commits listed in section 2

## 1. Summary

- The teacher has a **"Personalizar"** panel on the board, with **live preview**: board **name and description**, **logo**, **12 backgrounds** × dots / grid / plain, **layout** (Muro · Cuadrícula · Lista · **Por etapa** · **Por grupo**), **order** (joining · name · last updated) and **sticky header** (shrinks while scrolling). "Cancelar" discards the preview.
- Teachers wear the **#DOCENTE ring** (dark ring, gold label, graduation-cap badge): board header ("Docente: …"), user menu, Mi perfil, notes. Students never get it.
- **GSAP:** cards glide to their new place when the layout / order / stage filter changes (Flip); a classmate's new card pops in, an updated card glows; the stage ring draws itself; header and cards enter in a short stagger; background changes fade. All off with "reduce motion".
- **M13:** while presenting, the teacher opens **Notas** (key **N**) and writes on the slide on screen (Enter saves, Esc closes); a badge shows "2 notas". **"Vista del docente"** opens a second window (timer, next slide, notes) that stays in sync with the projected one.

## 6. Assumptions (change any of them)

- D25–D35 taken as proposed ("run loop 10").
- The teacher's name / photo in the header is copied when the teacher opens the board (or its admin page); after changing your photo, open the board once to refresh it.
- Groups are copied from the student list onto the cards when the teacher opens the board (no extra write when nothing changed). Students who joined after an import get their group on joining.
- The sticky header uses a simple scroll check (not ScrollTrigger): the board grows while it loads and live, which made precomputed scroll ranges unreliable.
- The console window syncs only within the same browser (BroadcastChannel); a phone as remote control is not included.
- The notes count badge is visible on the projected screen (no note text, only the number).

## 7. Issues / risks

- "Por etapa" / "Por grupo" on phones scroll sideways (snap per column).
- Free tier: no extra reads (look and teacher profiles travel on the board document); a logo ≈ 50 KB per board; Flip adds ≈ 10 KB gzip.
- Deferred from M11: admin CLI `usage` / `cleanup --avatars` (not needed yet).
